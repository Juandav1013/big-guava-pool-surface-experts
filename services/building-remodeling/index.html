From concept to coordinated scope

A successful pool belongs to the whole property.

New construction and major remodeling require more than choosing a shape or finish. The pool must connect to circulation, outdoor living, access, architecture and the way the property operates.

Big Guava’s planning conversation can include custom pool concepts, renovation direction, surface and tile selections, relevant equipment and the sequence needed to bring those pieces together. Major construction scopes are confirmed after reviewing the site and project requirements.

For an existing pool, remodeling may focus on a visual transformation or expand into tile, coping transitions, equipment and other connected improvements. The right scope depends on the condition of what is already there and the desired result.

Project questions

Decisions that shape the proposal.

Do you work on complete renovations?

Yes. The potential scope includes surface, tile, equipment and broader remodeling coordination. Final services depend on the property and project assessment.

Can the project start with design ideas only?

Yes. Early conversations can establish priorities, visual direction and feasibility considerations before selections are finalized.

Do you handle residential and commercial projects?

The brand serves both. Commercial projects may have property-specific operational, scheduling and compliance considerations that need separate review.

Are the website images completed Big Guava projects?

No. They are original conceptual visuals created for this website proposal and are clearly presented as design direction, not confirmed portfolio work.
