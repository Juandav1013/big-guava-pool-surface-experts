Discuss tile work
Tile has two jobs.

It manages the waterline while creating one of the most visible design moments in the pool.

Performance meets design

A small band with an outsized visual impact.

Pool tile frames the water, connects the interior finish to the surrounding deck and carries color across the entire perimeter.

Work may involve replacing loose or damaged tile, rebuilding selected areas, installing a completely new waterline or coordinating tile selection with resurfacing and remodeling. Substrate condition, existing dimensions and adjacent coping all influence the correct approach.

Material selection should account for suitability in a submerged or splash-zone environment, dimensional consistency, maintenance expectations and how the tile will read through the water.

Selection framework

Choose beyond the sample board.

  • Pool-rated material and installation suitability
  • Color under direct sun and through water
  • Scale relative to the pool geometry
  • Grout tone and maintenance expectations
  • Coping, deck and interior-finish coordination
  • Availability for future replacement pieces
Tile questions

Details worth resolving early.

Can only a few damaged tiles be replaced?

Sometimes. A localized repair depends on the surrounding bond, substrate condition and whether a reasonable visual match is available.

Should tile be replaced during resurfacing?

Not automatically, but resurfacing is an ideal time to evaluate the waterline because the transitions and work sequence can be coordinated.

Can ordinary wall tile be used in a pool?

Pool environments have specific exposure conditions. Material suitability should be confirmed rather than assumed from appearance alone.

Will new tile exactly match old tile?

Existing materials may be discontinued or weathered. A close match may be possible, but exact matching should not be promised without verification.

Give the waterline intention.

Share photos, the approximate pool size and whether tile work is part of a repair, resurfacing or full renovation.
