About Big Guava

Tampa-rooted. Surface-focused.

A pool repair and renovation brand built around the surfaces people see, touch and experience—and the connected systems that help the pool perform.

What the brand stands for

Start where condition and craftsmanship meet.

Big Guava Pool Surface Experts is positioned around a simple idea: the pool surface deserves specialist attention, and the complete renovation should still feel coordinated.

The service offering begins with resurfacing and targeted repairs, then extends into tile work, pumps, heaters, filtration and larger building or remodeling needs. This structure allows a project conversation to follow the actual condition of the pool rather than forcing every client into the same package.

The company serves residential and commercial clients. Project recommendations, materials, scheduling and the precise work scope are confirmed after reviewing the specific pool and property.
